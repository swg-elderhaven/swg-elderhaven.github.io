Adaptive Weapons & Weaponsmith ReworkHome

Implemented. Adaptive Weapons grow stronger the more you use them. Every hit grants weapon XP, and an Adaptive Weapon can reach Level 5000.

How the loop works

  1. Use an Adaptive Weapon and level it through combat.
  2. As it grows, it can generate Adaptive Fragments directly in your inventory.
  3. Higher weapon levels can produce higher-tier Fragments.
  4. Give or trade those materials to a Weaponsmith.
  5. Weaponsmith recipes have an additional Adaptive material slot, allowing substantially stronger crafted weapons with improved damage and secondary stats.
  6. Reach Level 5000 to receive an Adaptive Core, required for the highest-tier crafts.

There are four Fragment tiers, and drops can have different multipliers even within the same tier. Constantly leveling fresh low-level weapons will therefore not produce the best materials. High-level Adaptive Weapons remain valuable as long-term material generators for future weapons, alts, trading, and endgame crafting.

Fragments vs. Cores

Adaptive FragmentsBoost the power of crafted weapons.
Adaptive CoreUnlocks the highest tier of crafted Adaptive weapons.

Weaponsmiths and Adaptive Calibrators

To create an Adaptive Weapon, you must be at least a Novice Weaponsmith and obtain an Adaptive Calibrator from the Adaptive Blacksmith in Mos Eisley. The Calibrator works like a PUP: apply it to a weapon you crafted to convert that weapon into an Adaptive Weapon. You can then use it yourself or trade it to another player.

Weaponsmith only: Adaptive Calibrators do not work on basic Artisan-crafted weapons.

Jedi craft their own Adaptive Lightsabers and use a separate system.

Put your growing weapon to the test in Prestige Missions. Your character also develops separately through Insight / Prestige.

What if there are no Weaponsmiths around?

Adaptive Weapons are designed to remain strong on their own. Their Level 5000 damage is enough for the system to stand by itself. Weaponsmith crafting is an optional endgame layer for players who want to push weapon power further.

TL;DR: Adaptive Weapons level to 5000 through combat and generate four tiers of Adaptive Fragments along the way. Higher levels unlock better materials. Weaponsmiths can use those materials in an extra recipe slot to create much stronger weapons. Level 5000 awards an Adaptive Core for top-tier crafts. Adaptive Weapons work perfectly well without a Weaponsmith; crafting is the extra endgame path.
