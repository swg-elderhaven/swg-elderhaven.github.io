A Smuggler's LifeHome
Become a Lord or Lady of the Underworld. Start as a Master Smuggler looking for work and build your place in the criminal underworld. Traffic spice, negotiate dangerous deals and grow a network across the galaxy. Not every deal goes well.

A Smuggler's Life and Smuggler Runs are implemented for alpha testing. The main story leads into your own operations base, contacts and repeatable underworld work.

Where to start

Become a Master Smuggler, then find Loquito Quickshoot, a seasoned Rodian Smuggler outside the Coronet Spaceport on Corellia.

A place in the underworld

Quickshoot introduces you to SpyNet, spice dealers, fixers and criminals with their own interests. Investigation, Persuade, Intimidate and combat all have a place in the story.

Meet recurring characters, rescue a contact and decide whom to trust as your influence grows. Your choices lead into a criminal network of your own.

Your operations base

Advance through the story with Zapaya to earn access to the operations base. Its contacts and follow-up jobs develop your role as a Lord or Lady of the Underworld.

Access is earned through the story and retained across logouts. Qualifying leaders can also claim a premium black swoop from Giuseppe.

Smuggler Runs

Move product. Find the buyer. Try not to get shot. These repeatable jobs begin through your criminal contacts. Meet a supplier, acquire the shipment, follow the route to a buyer and report back to Zapaya.

Waypoints identify the destination and nearby spaceport to help you plan the trip. Suppliers and buyers remain out in the world, with varied appearances and personalities.

A purchase can turn hostile, attackers can ambush you on the road, and a buyer may decide to take the shipment by force. Your contacts can make those risks more interesting as your operations develop.

After completing a run, a return speeder offers a quick trip back. Repeatable Runs keep your network active beyond the main story.

Grow through the work

Supported story stages and missions award Adventure Insight XP, contributing to Insight / Prestige while you pursue your Smuggler path.

Alpha testing will refine rewards, encounter difficulty, attack chances and progression. The story and repeatable operations are already implemented; balance can change.
