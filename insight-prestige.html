Insight / Prestige SystemHome
Build your character by playing the world. Start with low base attributes, earn Insight XP through the activities you enjoy, and choose how your Health, Action and Mind grow. Prestige lets you rebuild with more choices available from the start.

Why Morrowind?

Elder Haven takes inspiration from the older RPG feeling of becoming stronger through life in the world. Combat, crafting, exploration and recurring characters all contribute to a character you build over time. Insight adds a separate progression system alongside your professions.

Starting small

New characters begin around 100 Health / 50 Action / 25 Mind, with racial differences. Your starting Insight System journal lets you spend available choices, individually or in bulk.

Look for creatures marked (Weakling) around starter cities. They are designed to help low-stat characters earn their first Insight levels. Visit an Assist Droid for starter support and buffs.

Six ways to earn Insight XP

These activities feed the same Insight progression:

  • Combat Insight XP: fighting enemies.
  • Crafting Insight XP: crafting, including practice crafting.
  • Scout Insight XP: harvesting creatures.
  • Entertainer Insight XP: performing flourishes while dancing or playing music.
  • Bounty Hunting Insight XP: completing bounty work.
  • Adventure Insight XP: completing missions and supported quest stages, including Haven's custom stories.

Insight XP is separate from profession XP. You can continue training professions even when your current Insight cycle is complete.

Choose your attributes

Each Insight level earns one choice. Spend it on:

  • Health: +20 Health, +4 Strength and +4 Constitution.
  • Action: +15 Action, +3 Quickness and +3 Stamina.
  • Mind: +10 Mind, +2 Focus and +2 Willpower.

Mix these however you want. Every allocated choice gives its full attribute increase, including stored choices.

Buffs grow with you

The potency of buffs you receive scales with the choices you have allocated. Choices sitting unspent in the journal do not count. Allocated stored choices also contribute, helping an experienced character start a new Prestige cycle stronger.

360 earned choices per cycle

Each cycle allows 360 earned choices. Once you reach that limit, you stop receiving Insight XP for the current cycle. The message "Prestige is ready" appears once, and your journal explains the next step.

Prestige is voluntary. Visit any Insight Master when your cycle is complete to review and confirm it. The initial alpha price is 100,000 credits per Prestige, with the same price for all five resets.

Stored choices: a stronger beginning

Prestige resets your current Insight level, XP and attribute allocations to the starting base. Your new cycle then has 100 stored choices for each Prestige rank, ready to allocate at Insight level 1.

Stored choices are additional to the 360 you can earn. Prestige 2 starts with 200 stored choices in total; each reset rebuilds that allowance from your new rank.

Prestige 5 is the maximum. You can complete its final 360-choice cycle for a total of 860 choices. Insight progression then stops; there is no sixth Prestige.

Your other progress stays

You keep your professions, equipment, quests and Adaptive progression. Credits are retained apart from the displayed Prestige fee. Cancelling the confirmation leaves your character unchanged; insufficient funds prevent the reset.

Journal and Insight Masters

Use the journal to allocate choices and review your Insight progress. Insight Masters handle Prestige and the separate paid respec service. A respec lets you redistribute choices while keeping your current Insight XP and cycle progress.

For tougher combat and faster Insight gains, see Prestige Missions. For equipment bonuses and defenses, see Professions & Combat.

These are the initial alpha progression rules. The Prestige fee, rewards and balance values may be adjusted during testing.
