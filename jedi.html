Follow a story into the Force. Elder Haven combines profession mastery and exploration with a custom quest, then continues into the familiar Force Sensitive, Padawan and Jedi Knight progression.

Qualifying for the path

Develop your character through Master professions and exploration badges, then complete An Echo in the Sand. This custom story replaces the old themepark requirement for Haven's Jedi unlock route.

Exact mastery and exploration requirements will be confirmed with the alpha setup instructions and may be adjusted during testing.

An Echo in the Sand

A dying Twi'lek in Tatooine asks you to find her sister. Following that request draws you into a personal story of investigation, dangerous encounters and unfinished business. The quest has its own journal and contributes Adventure Insight XP.

Where to begin: Travel to Tatooine, then enter /waypoint 4080 -4550 in chat. Follow the waypoint and look nearby for Zyanna, the dying Twi'lek. Speak to her to begin the quest once you meet the entry requirements.

Complete the story to fulfill this part of the Jedi unlock requirements. The rest is yours to discover.

The classic progression, at your pace

After qualifying, continue through the Old Man encounter and Force Sensitive progression. Long artificial waits and Village phase delays are removed.

At the Village, complete the required Force Sensitive training, face Mellichae and proceed to the Padawan Trials. Individual Force Sensitive branches do not require waiting for rotating Village phases.

Padawan and Jedi Knight Trials retain their native structure. The three Padawan hunting trials have nearby group objectives and waypoints, with saved progress that lets you resume unfinished hunts.

Becoming a Padawan

Completing the real Padawan transition removes your previous non-Force professions and commits your character to the Jedi path. Jedi and Force Sensitive training remain available; ordinary profession trainers refuse further training.

Your Insight, Prestige, equipment and completed story progress remain. Profession-dependent Mandalorian and Smuggler activities require their own skills, so consider this commitment before becoming a Padawan.

Training equipment

The Training Lightsaber has two crystal slots, allowing a color crystal and your Padawan Crystal together. Tune the Padawan Crystal normally before inserting it.

The Padawan crystal and robe are balanced as starter equipment. The robe provides modest defenses and Jedi Toughness, with no Saber Block bonus. Stronger equipment and further Jedi progression lie ahead.

Jedi is intended to be the alpha path. Elder Haven is primarily balanced around PvE, with separate PvP tuning. Power, progression speed and encounter difficulty will be refined during alpha.

Read Professions & Combat for the rules on defenses and equipment bonuses, and Insight / Prestige for long-term attribute growth.
