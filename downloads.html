There's no Elder Haven patch to download yet. A compatible Pre-CU SWG client is all you need, with the connection details below.

Why there are no downloads yet

Everything custom on Elder Haven currently runs on the server and uses assets already in the client. That includes Insight / Prestige, the quests and the combat changes. You don't need an Elder Haven TRE patch or a separate launcher for them.

You do need a working, compatible Pre-CU Star Wars Galaxies client. If you already have one, add Elder Haven to its login list.

Adding Elder Haven to your login list

Create a new login entry in your client setup tool with these details:

More content and a launcher

I plan to keep adding to Elder Haven during alpha and beyond. That includes more custom quests, encounters, mission objectives and other content.

An Elder Haven launcher is also planned for when custom client files are added, to make setup and updates easier. It isn't available yet. When it's ready, the launcher, downloads and instructions will go on this page.

The server is open in alpha. You can join Discord for updates, help getting connected and bug reports.

Once you're connected, the new player guide covers your first Insight levels, buffs and missions.
