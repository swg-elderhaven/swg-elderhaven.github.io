Professions & CombatHome
Build a powerful character across professions. Elder Haven lets ordinary characters master all standard combat professions, combine equipment bonuses and develop their attributes through Insight. The Jedi path has its own profession restrictions.

Grand Master: expand your skill capacity

Your character starts with the standard 250 skill points. After mastering an eligible profession, visit a Grand Master Teacher to turn further XP in that profession's field into additional skill capacity. You must still hold its Master skill and mastery badge when you claim it.

With the initial alpha settings, each Grand Mastery costs 1,500,000 profession XP and grants up to 1,000 additional skill points, with a total capacity limit of 10,000. You receive the new points ready to spend, so you can keep developing other professions.

Each eligible profession can be claimed once per character. All Grand Master Teachers share your progress; visiting another Teacher does not grant the same reward again. Eligible fields include combat, scouting and support professions such as Doctor, Combat Medic, Dancer and Musician. Crafting professions do not award additional Grand Masteries.

Teachers are available in starter cities. Ask one "How high is my skill cap?" to check your capacity and free points. Insight Prestige preserves your professions and Grand Master progress. Jedi still follows its own profession restrictions.

All combat professions

Train and master the standard combat professions you want. You gain the skills and bonuses from the boxes you learn, while combat calculations still use the modifiers appropriate to your equipped weapon and the action you perform.

Becoming a Padawan removes your previous non-Force professions and commits you to Jedi and Force Sensitive progression. Insight, Prestige, gear and completed quest progress stay with your character.

Armor and clothing attachments

The old +25 aggregate equipment-bonus limit has been removed. Bonuses from skill tapes, Armor Attachments and Clothing Attachments can add together across compatible pieces you actually wear.

Normal equipment slots, wearable restrictions and attachment-application rules still apply. An item in your inventory does not contribute a worn bonus.

Can I apply the same tape repeatedly?

The same modifier on one item keeps its stronger value. Applying a +15 tape over the same +10 modifier leaves that item at +15.

Across different equipped pieces, bonuses add together. Five compatible pieces with +10 in the same supported modifier can contribute +50 equipment bonus, before the combat rules for that stat are applied.

Defenses and diminishing returns

Additional defense bonuses continue to help at high totals, with smaller effective gains beyond the defense threshold. This gives profession mastery, buffs and equipment value while preserving difficult encounters.

A defense score is not a percentage chance to avoid damage. Toughness, Saber Block, Force protection and hit chances also have their own limits. Combining professions does not automatically guarantee immunity.

The Insight System journal includes a combat-stat readout showing accumulated and effective values. Use it to understand your current character, including equipment and active modifiers.

The alpha balance goal

Jedi is intended to be the alpha path. Ordinary characters and Mandalorians can also become very powerful through professions, attachments, Insight and Adaptive gear.

The server is primarily tuned for PvE, with separate PvP controls. Alpha feedback will shape damage, defenses, rewards and progression speed. High-power characters have Prestige Missions to challenge their builds.

These rules describe the initial alpha balance pass. Numerical thresholds and percentages may change during testing.
