Welcome to the Haven!Server Features

These are the systems that define Elder Haven. Elder Haven aims to capture the feeling of an older RPG, where your choices help shape the world. We will keep building the server around that idea. Progression comes from exploring the world, discovering its systems and committing to long character paths through quests, earned rewards and new opportunities.

◆

Insight / Prestige

Combat, crafting, harvesting, entertaining, bounty hunting and quests build your attributes. Earn 360 choices per cycle, then Prestige at an Insight Master to restart with stored choices. Each rank adds 100 stored choices, up to Prestige 5.

▣

Retro-RPG faction stories

Custom questlines are closer in spirit to Morrowind-style faction progression than disposable MMO tasks. You meet recurring characters, build your place inside a group and can eventually become part of its leadership, such as becoming leader of the Mandalorians or a Lord or Lady of the Underworld.

▧

Adaptive Weapons & Weaponsmith Rework

Adaptive weapons grow through combat up to Level 5000 and generate tiered crafting materials as they develop. Weaponsmiths can use those fragments and cores to push crafted weapons far beyond their normal limits.

╱

Classic Jedi, less waiting

Profession mastery and exploration lead into An Echo in the Sand, a custom story replacing the themepark requirement. Continue through Force Sensitive training and the Padawan Trials, with Village phase waiting removed.

!

Prestige Missions

A separate P0-P5 terminal selector reinforces familiar enemies beyond normal mission difficulty. Higher tiers offer more Insight XP and credits, with a serious challenge for advanced characters.

+

Professions, tapes & defenses

Master all standard combat professions and combine attachment bonuses across equipped pieces. The old +25 equipment-bonus ceiling is removed; defenses use diminishing returns. Jedi follows a separate profession path.

⚒

More time in the world

Faster profession progression, four terminal missions at once, directional mission requests and reduced travel waiting help you spend more time playing the world.

Your First StepsNew Player Guide

Start with the client connection guide to add Elder Haven to your login list. Once you enter:

  1. Open your Insight System journal. You start with low base attributes and build them through play.
  2. Visit an Assist Droid for starter support and buffs.
  3. Find (Weakling) creatures around a starter city to earn your first Insight levels.
  4. Spend choices in the journal on Health, Action or Mind. Buff potency grows with allocated choices.
  5. Train professions, take missions and follow stories. Insight also comes from crafting, harvesting, entertaining and bounty work.

Begin with normal missions and raise mission Prestige as your build develops. At 360 earned choices, visit an Insight Master for the voluntary Prestige reset.
