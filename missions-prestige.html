Prestige MissionsHome
Choose how far to push your character. Prestige Missions reinforce the familiar destroy-mission enemies, creating tougher encounters for advanced Insight, Adaptive gear and Prestige progression.

Two independent selectors

Mission terminals offer mission difficulty and a separate mission Prestige selector, P0 through P5. Difficulty selects from the terminal's normal mission options; Prestige determines how heavily those enemies are reinforced.

You can use Prestige even at the terminal's maximum normal difficulty. The selector is available to everyone; your own Prestige rank does not lock higher tiers.

P0 is normal; higher tiers are a challenge

P0 generates normal missions. P1-P5 use the usual creature types with stronger attributes, more damage and increased accuracy. The higher the tier, the more demanding the encounter.

A fully developed Prestige 1 character may still be overwhelmed by Prestige 3 enemies. Choose a tier your current character and equipment can handle, then move upward as you grow.

Insight XP and credits

Higher tiers award more Insight XP and more credits. They provide a faster progression option when your character is ready for the challenge, and a use for the power earned through Insight / Prestige and Adaptive Weapons.

Credit rewards will be tuned during alpha alongside the wider economy.

Your accepted contract stays consistent

Each offered contract records its tier and scaling. Changing your terminal filters later does not change the challenge or rewards of a contract already accepted. Refresh the terminal to request new offers with your selected settings.

Other mission improvements

  • Carry up to four terminal mission contracts at once, including at most one active bounty.
  • Select destroy-mission difficulty within the terminal's available spawn range.
  • Request missions by direction to plan your route.

Learn how gear bonuses and defenses work in Professions & Combat.

Enemy power and rewards are initial alpha values. Dedicated Prestige creatures may be added as future content.
