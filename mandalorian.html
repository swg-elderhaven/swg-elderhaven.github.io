Mandalorian PathHome
Become the leader of the Mandalorians. Earn your place piece by piece, build armor that grows through use, master exclusive technology and take command of the Mandalorian Enclave.

The Mandalorian Path combines a custom questline, Adaptive Mandalorian Armor, Fuel-powered abilities and leadership.

Starting the path

Become a Master Bounty Hunter and speak with Peshisho in Mos Eisley to begin the Mandalorian questline. Continue along the path to reach Chorizon's leadership story.

Earn the armor piece by piece

Chorizon knows of powerful figures holding pieces of an unusual suit of beskar armor. Track down each contact, decide how to approach the encounter, and return to Chorizon as the story advances.

Some encounters leave room for conversation; others lead to combat. Armor pieces are awarded progressively through the questline, so completing the suit is part of your journey.

Adaptive Mandalorian Armor

The armor begins at 1% of its stats and grows stronger through use, including its lightsaber resistance. Regular Mandalorian Armor can still be crafted normally; the Adaptive version is earned through the Mandalorian Path.

Fuel and exclusive technology

Progress far enough along the path to craft and use the Personal Shield Generator and Micro Missile. Their consumable devices require resources to craft, so keeping your equipment supplied is part of being a Mandalorian.

Both abilities draw from a dedicated Fuel bar. Its initial capacity is 100 units, regenerating at 1 unit per second while you carry a Fuel resource. Any Fuel resource type works.

Lead the Enclave

Completing the leadership path grants control of the Mandalorian Enclave. Dispatch your Mandalorians on missions for rewards; the initial interval is 12 hours.

Build your character further through Insight / Prestige, and challenge your equipment in Prestige Missions.

Armor growth, Fuel values, ability balance and Enclave mission intervals may be adjusted during alpha.
