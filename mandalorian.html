Mandalorian PathHome
You can become leader of the Mandalorians. The questline takes you through earning your armor, using Mandalorian technology and eventually taking charge of the Enclave. Your armor also grows stronger as you use it.

The path has its own questline, Adaptive Mandalorian Armor, abilities that use Fuel and an Enclave you can eventually lead.

Getting started

Become a Master Bounty Hunter, then speak to Peshisho in Mos Eisley to start. Continuing through the quests leads to Chorizon's leadership story.

Collecting the armor

Chorizon knows several powerful people who each hold a piece of an unusual beskar suit. You'll track them down, decide how to deal with them and return to Chorizon as the story moves along.

Some encounters can be handled through conversation, while others involve fighting. You earn the armor pieces as you go through the questline, gradually putting the suit together.

Adaptive Mandalorian Armor

The armor starts at 1% of its stats and grows stronger as you use it, including its lightsaber resistance. You can still craft regular Mandalorian Armor as usual. This Adaptive version comes from the questline.

Shields, missiles and Fuel

Later in the path, you unlock crafting and use of the Personal Shield Generator and Micro Missile. The devices are consumable and cost resources to craft, so you'll need to keep making supplies.

Both abilities use a separate Fuel bar. It currently holds 100 units and regenerates 1 unit per second while you carry a Fuel resource. Any type of Fuel resource works.

Leading the Enclave

Finishing the leadership path puts you in charge of the Mandalorian Enclave. You can send your Mandalorians out on missions for rewards, currently once every 12 hours.

You can keep improving your character with Insight / Prestige and take your gear into Prestige Missions for harder fights.

Armor growth, Fuel, abilities and the Enclave mission timer may change as people test them during alpha.
